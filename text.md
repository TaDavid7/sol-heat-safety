Challenge: Invisible Interfaces
​The best interface is sometimes no interface at all. This edition explores products that work through voice, motion, sensors, live data, autonomous agents, and other natural interactions.

​Build technology that helps people or systems sense, decide, and act without relying on a traditional app or screen.

​1. Dispatch
​Use live data such as ship traffic, flight data, fleet GPS, sensors, or border wait times to move physical things where they need to go.

​Build for logistics, transportation, ports, emergency response, or defense.

​2. No App
​Build for people who will never open an app, including seniors, drivers, nurses, warehouse workers, and field crews.

​Use voice, phone calls, glasses, vibration, or another invisible interface. If users have to look at a screen, it does not count.

​3. Unsupervised
​Build agents that run for hours without constant supervision.

​Your system should know when it is stuck, overspending, looping, or when it needs to stop.

​4. AI-Powered Consumer Products for 1 Billion People
​Build an AI-powered consumer product that reaches people through natural, ambient, or invisible interactions.

​Rethink how people discover, use, and benefit from technology beyond traditional apps and screens. The strongest ideas will address a clear everyday need and have the potential to serve people at global scale.
